Before you write about the data

Candidate names, races, and party labels are transcribed from the Texas Secretary of State’s certified ballot reports for the November 3, 2026 general election in Tarrant County, Dallas County, and Collin County. If a name here differs from the report for its county, it is our transcription error and worth reporting. If the report itself is wrong, the correction has to go through the county elections office — this guide follows the record rather than departing from it.
